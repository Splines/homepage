2. At a glance

This is a small personal blog. It is deliberately built to collect as little data as possible:

  • No advertising and no cross-site tracking. There is no Google Analytics, no Matomo, no tracking pixel, no advertising network, and no profiling. A privacy-focused, cookieless reach measurement by my hosting provider is active; it is described in section 5.
  • No cookies. This website sets no cookies of its own and stores no data in your browser's local storage. The reach measurement in section 5 also works without cookies. Cookies may only be set by GitHub inside the comment section described in section 6, if you load it.
  • No user accounts and no newsletter.
  • All fonts are hosted on this server. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any comparable service.
  • Two third parties are nevertheless involved: my hosting provider (Cloudflare), and the comment system on blog posts (giscus / GitHub). Both are described below.

Legal bases used

Providing personal data is neither legally nor contractually required. You can read this website without providing any data beyond what is technically necessary to deliver the pages to you.

4. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, a service provided by Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA ("Cloudflare"). Cloudflare also acts as a content delivery network, meaning that the pages are delivered to you from a server geographically close to you.

When you access this website, your browser necessarily transmits technical data to Cloudflare's servers, which is processed in server log files. This typically includes:

  • your IP address
  • the date and time of the request
  • the page or file requested
  • the HTTP status code and amount of data transferred
  • the referring URL, if any
  • your browser type, version and operating system

This data is processed on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in delivering this website reliably, protecting it against attacks and abuse, and ensuring its technical stability. This data is not merged with other data sources, and I do not use it to identify individual visitors.

Cloudflare additionally processes this data to provide security functions such as protection against denial-of-service attacks. Details are set out in Cloudflare's own privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

A data processing agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR exists with Cloudflare: Cloudflare's Customer Data Processing Addendum is incorporated by reference into the subscription agreement under which this website is hosted.

Encryption

This website uses TLS encryption (recognisable by the https:// prefix and the padlock icon in your browser) to protect the transmission of content between your browser and the server.

How it is embedded

Cloudflare inserts the measurement script automatically into the delivered pages of the production website. The script (beacon.min.js) is retrieved from static.cloudflareinsights.com, and the measurement data is transmitted to Cloudflare. As the insertion takes place on Cloudflare's servers while the page is being delivered, the script is not part of this website's source code.

What is recorded

According to Cloudflare, each page view records:

  • the address of the page called up, without any query parameters
  • the referring page, if any
  • browser type and version and the operating system, derived from the user agent
  • the country, derived from your IP address
  • the HTTP status code of the response
  • loading and performance measurements provided by your browser's performance interface (for example how long the page took to become visible and usable)
  • a randomly generated identifier that is valid only for this single page view and is not stored afterwards

What is not done

According to Cloudflare, this service sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and uses no fingerprinting. Your IP address is evaluated only transiently in order to determine the country and is not stored. Cloudflare states that it does not track individual users across the websites of its customers, and the data is not used to build a profile of you. I myself see only aggregated statistics and cannot identify individual visitors.

Legal basis and your options

The processing takes place on the basis of Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in a needs-based design and the technical optimisation of this website, measured in a way that avoids cookies and personal profiles. You may object to this processing at any time under Art. 21 GDPR, using the contact details in section 1.

Because the script is inserted by Cloudflare during delivery, it cannot be deselected within this website. You can prevent it from being executed by using a content blocker in your browser or by disabling JavaScript. Further information can be found in Cloudflare's privacy policy and in the Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation.

What happens, and when

Nothing is loaded from giscus or GitHub until you ask for it. Below each blog post you will find a notice with a "Load comments" button. As long as you do not click it, no connection to giscus.app or to GitHub is established and no data is transmitted to those providers.

Only when you click the button is the comment section loaded and embedded as an iframe served by giscus and GitHub. From that moment on, your IP address, your browser data and the address of the page you are viewing are transmitted to those providers. If you are logged in to GitHub in the same browser, GitHub can associate the retrieval with your GitHub account. Cookies and comparable technologies may be set by GitHub within that iframe; these originate from GitHub, not from me, and I have no access to them.

Your decision applies to the page you are currently viewing and is not stored. If you open another blog post, or reload the page, the comments will again only be loaded once you click.

Posting a comment

Writing a comment requires a GitHub account and your authorisation of the giscus application. The comment, your GitHub username and your avatar then become publicly visible in the associated GitHub discussion and on this website. Please bear in mind that comments are public and can be read and indexed by anyone.

Comments are stored by GitHub for as long as the discussion exists. You can edit or delete your own comments at any time via the GitHub discussion, or ask me to remove them.

Legal basis

By clicking the "Load comments" button you consent to the described transmission of data to giscus and GitHub, and to the storage of and access to information on your device by GitHub within the embedded comment section. The legal basis is therefore your consent pursuant to Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR and § 25 (1) TDDDG.

You may withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future, simply by reloading or leaving the page: as your decision is not stored, the comment section will not be loaded again unless you click the button once more. The lawfulness of the processing carried out up to the withdrawal is not affected.

Further information can be found in the giscus privacy policy and in GitHub's privacy statement.
